# EmuForwarder 1.0.0: promo video, voice-over script

**Video:** `EmuForwarder-promo.mp4`, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 1:34. It is built by `make_promo.py` (see the top of that file).
**Music:** "Twilight Game Menu" by Siarhei Korbut (Pixabay Content License: free to use, no attribution required). It plays at full level until the voice-over starts, then sits about 10 dB under the voice.
**Voice:** calm, friendly, unhurried, about 140 words per minute. Each line fits its window with a little room to breathe. The video already has on-screen captions, so it also works without a voice.

The times are when each line **starts** and the latest it should **end** (mm:ss). The roadmap items appear at 1:13, 1:17 and 1:21, so put each sentence there.

## Delivery notes

- **Pronunciation:** say "one point oh" (not "one zero zero"). "EmuSuNi" is *emu-SOO-nee*. "Itemzflow" is *items-flow*.
- **Pace:** smile on lines 7 and 12. Slow down on "No menus in between."
- **Timing:** record each line as its own take, and line them up with the start times above.
- **Music:** duck it under the voice, about 10 dB, with a 0.3 s fade in and out around each line.
